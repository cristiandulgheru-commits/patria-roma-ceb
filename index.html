Annex A — Roma communities (extract)

Business development for Roma communities in Romania

Technical assistance aligned with the CEB blending facility: coupling debt financing for vulnerable groups with business development services. This page reflects only the Roma community strand — PATRIA Credit & REDI.

Why Roma communities are in focus

Within Romania, Roma communities face limited access to resources and opportunities, including for entrepreneurship. Many people have had limited educational opportunities and struggle to develop viable, scalable businesses. Enterprises often operate in the informal economy and may not meet formalisation requirements. Systematic discrimination and lack of appropriate microfinance and mentoring further constrain success.

Activity Group A — Roma businesses

Activity Group A covers businesses based in Roma communities, with two sub-segments: start-up and informal businesses, and established businesses with the potential to scale.

  • Start-up / informal: entrepreneurs need clarity on formalisation, sound business models, quality and consistent products, and identifiable market opportunities — including digital channels.
  • Growth-oriented firms: training and coaching on standards, regulations and certifications; links with larger buyers and partners; market access strategies; practical growth skills. Includes Roma Digital boost (e-commerce).

Programme targets (beneficiaries)

Group A — start-up / informal 275

Aspiring entrepreneurs and informal businesses supported to formally establish a business from a viable idea.

Group A — existing businesses with growth potential 325

Established businesses supported on growth strategy, quality / certification, and new markets.

How delivery is organised

REDI coordinates activities for participants from Roma communities, including outreach, culturally sensitive approaches, and the Roma Team coordinator role (community leaders, workshops, feedback). Patria Credit coordinates smallholder-farmer strands. Capacity building covers module development and quality delivery; field-based support is emphasised alongside trust-building where needed.
